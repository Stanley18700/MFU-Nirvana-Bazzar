One-line: names an activity, zone or track the way the poster does — a flat torn scrap of colour, slightly askew.

```jsx
<ScrapLabel color="var(--scrap-terracotta)" index={0} tape>Cultural Workshops</ScrapLabel>
<ScrapLabel color="var(--orange-400)" textColor="var(--ink-900)" index={1}>Youth Booth</ScrapLabel>
```

Tilt comes only from `--tilt-scrap-a/b/c` — three fixed angles, so a row of labels looks hand-placed rather than random.
Keep the text short: two or three words. Never use a scrap colour for a button or any other chrome.
