One-line: the festival's hero headline — only ever on the sky ground or the key visual, never on white.

```jsx
<PosterHeading kicker="Experience the world in one place!"
  sub="16–18 September 2026  |  Mae Fah Luang University">
  Bridging cultures, building global citizens
</PosterHeading>
```

White display type with the hard shadow needs a sky-500 or darker ground to hold contrast.
On white, use a plain heading in `--ink-900` instead.
