One-line: inline messages — write them as one plain sentence the reader can act on.

```jsx
<Notice>Reach 50 points and your redemption code appears here.</Notice>
<Notice tone="warning">Offline — showing the last saved copy of the leaderboard.</Notice>
<Notice tone="danger" dark>You are not allowed to read this booth. Sign out and back in.</Notice>
```

No icons, no borders, no exclamation marks. `dark` is not decorative — the light tones are unreadable on the green grounds.
