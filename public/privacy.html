Privacy notice · MFU Go Global Passport

Draft for review by the Office of International Affairs before the event.

What we collect

Your display name, visitor type, optional student/staff ID, institution and school, country of origin, one contact (phone or email), the time you check in at each booth, and — only if you separately consent — your self-identified ethnic group.

Why

To run the stamp-collecting activity and prize redemption, and to report aggregate participation figures for the festival. Ethnic group is used only in aggregate, with groups under five people folded into "Other", to show that the festival reached MFU's multi-ethnic community.

Who sees it

Event administrators from the School of Applied Digital Technology and the Office of International Affairs. Booth staff see only visitor counts. Your ethnic group is never shown alongside your name to anyone.

Where and how long

Data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the Singapore region and personal fields are erased 90 days after the event (by 17 December 2026). Anonymous aggregate statistics are kept.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act you may withdraw consent, ask for a copy, or ask for erasure at any time from your passport page or by contacting the School of Applied Digital Technology.
